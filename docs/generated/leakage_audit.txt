  The permutation preserves every marginal and destroys only the
  pairing. The test is whether 'permuted' matches 'null', not 0.5.

  NOTE, and it is a defect in the metric rather than a leak: a
  feature of exactly zero is counted as a wrong answer. Tied queues
  are common, so this biases queue imbalance DOWNWARD -- its
  accuracy is measured against a ceiling of 1 - P(f=0), not 1.
